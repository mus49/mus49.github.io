Inline Math

The quadratic formula is $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$ which solves $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$.

Einstein’s famous equation is $E = mc^2$.

Display Math (Block)

The Pythagorean theorem:

$$ a^2 + b^2 = c^2 $$

Euler’s identity:

$$ e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0 $$

A more complex example with summation:

$$ \sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2} $$

Matrix notation:

$$ \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} ax + by \\ cx + dy \end{bmatrix} $$

Greek Letters and Symbols

Alpha $\alpha$, Beta $\beta$, Gamma $\gamma$, Delta $\delta$, Theta $\theta$, Lambda $\lambda$, Sigma $\sigma$, Omega $\omega$

Integrals: $\int_0^\infty e^{-x^2} dx = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}$

Limits: $\lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{1}{x} = 0$
