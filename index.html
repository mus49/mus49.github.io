I'm a research master's student at IIIT Delhi, working in NLP. My thesis is on the computational analysis of propaganda and persuasion — how persuasive intent is organised into narratives, and how reliably language models can be made to recognise it.

More recently I've been pulled toward the interpretability side of that question: what a model's internal representations encode about a construct, and how much of it survives being put into words.

Research interests

  • Computational propaganda, persuasion, and narrative framing
  • Interpretability: probing internal representations, self-report vs. internal state
  • Optimization methods for machine learning
